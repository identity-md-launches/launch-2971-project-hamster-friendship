  • Each map has 30 enemies and one boss. The boss appears at the forest exit after 60 seconds of active play.
  • One unblocked enemy hit or a fall restarts the current map. There is no healing. Shield blocks and stomps still work.
  • Every defeated enemy drops one gold SQK coin. Walk over it to keep it. Coins left behind disappear when you die.
  • Map 1 enemies have 100 HP; its boss has 500 HP. Each deeper map has 20% more HP, rounded up.
  • Weapon drop chance: 30%. Of drops, 30% are guns (20 damage), 30% swords (50 damage), 40% grenades (5 burning damage per second in an area for 8 seconds).
  • Chestnuts deal 25 damage; stomps deal 50. Shells fall from the canopy when supplies run low. Lanterns give spare shells.
  • Raise your shield when the cockroach charges. A blocked charge leaves it dazed.
  • Defeat all 30 enemies and the boss, then inspect the metal fragment beyond the gate to reach the next map.
  • Score: 100 per regular enemy, 500 per boss, 1,000 per map cleared. Cloud scores are checked by the server. Guest scores stay on this device.

The story so far

In a peaceful village beneath a chestnut forest, two hamster friends, Alice and Chang, live together.

One evening a mysterious beam of light strikes the village. When Chang arrives, Alice is gone. All that is left is her overturned basket and a trail of scattered chestnut shells.

Follow the trail, look for clues and find what lies at the forest exit.
